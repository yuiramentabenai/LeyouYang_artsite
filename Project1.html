Game intro

Dizzilusional is a narrative puzzle game. The project focuses on the conflicts of social expectation and personal life choices. Iteration of rights develops while time goes by. The myth which the game rooted from is the story of "Wuhuaka". The myth is a fundamental shift point of marriage in southwestern China. Aiming to construct the connection of players' interactions and the result of iteration, the project is created, highlighting environmental narrative and cultural representations.

Main Character

A mediocre researcher who is studying medicine. In her twenties, she lives in a time when peers are getting married, while she is not on that track yet. Her mother pushes social expectations on her, warning of terrible endings for those who do not marry.

Iteration—people’s life choices evolving through time—is central to the story. The main character is from the modern world, while what she tries to change belongs to the era of the myth. Two versions of the heroine were designed.
