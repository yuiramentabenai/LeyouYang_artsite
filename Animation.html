Left Behind

Urbex is an activity in which people explore abandoned places. The reasons for abandonment are usually unexpectedly mundane. However, one collective characteristic of popular abandoned sites is that people often left in a rush, leaving behind a large number of personal items. Fragments of memories are embedded within these “left-behinds.” Therefore, the project is meant to visualize this experience.he experience visualized. UE5 was used for rendeering.
